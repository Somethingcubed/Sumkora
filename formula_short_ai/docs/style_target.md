# Style Target: Formula Launch Film

Use these as the visual north star for every AI-generated clip:

- Color master: `assets/style_targets/formula_launch_realistic_color.png`
- Black-and-white master: `assets/style_targets/formula_launch_realistic_bw.png`
- Earlier reference kept for comparison: `assets/style_targets/formula_launch_style_target.png`

## Core Look

- Premium sneaker / fragrance / car launch commercial, not museum, classroom, chalkboard, or explainer.
- Formulas are thick physical 3D product objects with bevels, shadows, reflections, and mass.
- The image should feel photographed in a real black studio, not rendered in a game engine.
- Use wet black reflective flooring, black void background, smoke/haze, practical light tubes.
- Include grounded shadows, imperfect reflections, micro-scratches, fingerprints, dust, subtle film grain.
- Hard white rim lights plus selective red/cyan accents; some shots can be pure black-and-white.
- Ultra-wide low camera angles, Dutch tilts, snap zooms, whip pans, speed ramps.
- Material language: chrome, brushed metal, black rubber, ceramic, acrylic, carbon fiber, translucent gel.

## Master Prompt Add-On

Append this to every image-to-video prompt if the tool allows long prompts:

> Make this feel like a high-end sneaker, fragrance, or car launch commercial photographed in a real black studio. The formula is the hero product: a thick physical 3D object with beveled reflective material, not flat text. Use a wet black reflective studio floor, black void background, practical light tubes, smoke/haze, hard rim lights, ultra-wide low-angle camera, Dutch tilt, snap zoom, speed ramp, realistic shadows, imperfect reflections, micro-scratches, fingerprints, dust, subtle film grain, and expensive commercial cinematography. Avoid artificial plastic CGI, game-render smoothness, museum, classroom, chalkboard, flat poster, or educational explainer.

## Shot Modes

- Color hero: use `formula_launch_realistic_color.png` as the style reference.
- Black-and-white impact: use `formula_launch_realistic_bw.png`; keep deep blacks, bright rim lights, wet reflections, and smoke.
- Macro detail: push close lens, material imperfections, bevel highlights, shallow depth of field.
- Finale: combine color and monochrome language, but keep the same black studio product campaign world.

## Clip Selection Rule

Keep clips where the formula feels like a physical object in a real studio, even if the exact symbols are slightly less perfect. Reject clips that are readable but flat, overly clean, plasticky, game-like, or educational.
