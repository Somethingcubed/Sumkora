# Simulation Exit Protocol Character Bible

Use this file to cast and lock the main characters in Artlist Studio before generating scene shots. Create one clean reference image per character first, then reuse the same character tag/name across all prompts.

## Artlist Studio Prompting Notes

Artlist Studio is designed around production stages such as casting characters, building locations, framing scenes, and directing shots. Its Studio page describes character casting as defining age, physical presence, look, and mood, and it supports prompt tags, character/location capture, and cross-scene consistency. For this short, the practical workflow is:

- Create each character as a clean reference portrait first.
- Save or tag the character by name inside Artlist Studio.
- Keep wardrobe, hair, age, and facial structure stable in every shot.
- Change only expression, pose, lighting, and scene context.
- Use realistic modern casting language rather than celebrity names.

## Global Character Style

All characters should feel like real people in a grounded psychological thriller. Avoid perfect fashion-model faces, glossy influencer styling, fantasy costumes, futuristic uniforms, exaggerated sci-fi makeup, or extreme horror expressions. Faces should have natural asymmetry, skin texture, small imperfections, and believable fatigue.

Use this add-on for all character reference prompts:

> realistic cinematic character reference, natural human face, subtle skin texture, believable imperfections, grounded modern psychological thriller, neutral background, soft cinematic lighting, 16:9 horizontal frame, sharp eyes, no celebrity likeness, no fantasy styling, no exaggerated makeup

## Oscar

### Character Function

Oscar is the audience's entry point. He begins ordinary, bored, and skeptical, then becomes increasingly alert as reality misbehaves. His fear should not feel theatrical; it should look like someone trying very hard to stay rational while his body already knows something is wrong.

### Personality

- Restless, intelligent, and quietly lonely.
- Uses sarcasm and denial as self-protection.
- Curious enough to click the stream, cautious enough to doubt it.
- Not heroic in a flashy way; his courage is desperation plus honesty.
- His emotional arc moves from numb boredom to rationalization, then fear, then a fragile kind of hope.

### Physical Outline

- Age: early 20s.
- Build: slim, average height, slightly hunched from too much screen time.
- Face: narrow oval face, tired eyes, faint under-eye shadows, expressive eyebrows, slightly chapped lips.
- Hair: messy dark brown or black hair, medium-short, unstyled, falls forward slightly.
- Skin: light-to-medium natural skin tone, realistic texture.
- Wardrobe at home: pale gray T-shirt, dark sweatpants or casual shorts, bare feet or socks.
- Wardrobe outside: dark casual jacket over the gray T-shirt, black backpack, worn sneakers.
- Body language: restless hands, guarded shoulders, quick glances, breath held when afraid.

## Professor Serena Voss

### Character Function

Serena is the story's most controlled presence. She should be believable as a serious professor, but there is something unsettling in how little she reacts. She can seem compassionate and manipulative at the same time. The audience should never be fully sure whether she is rescuing Oscar, studying him, or enjoying the experiment.

### Personality

- Brilliant, patient, and unnervingly calm.
- Speaks with careful precision, never rushing.
- Compassionate on the surface, but emotionally distant.
- Comfortable with fear because she has seen it many times.
- Possesses the quiet confidence of someone who knows more layers than everyone else.
- Final reveal should make her earlier calm feel more sinister in retrospect.

### Physical Outline

- Age: late 40s to early 50s.
- Build: tall or medium-tall, slim, upright posture.
- Face: angular cheekbones, sharp attentive eyes, controlled mouth, slight age lines.
- Hair: silver-blonde bob, precise and neat, chin-length or slightly shorter.
- Skin: fair-to-light natural skin tone, realistic mature texture.
- Wardrobe: dark tailored blazer, simple blouse or black turtleneck, minimal jewelry.
- Body language: still hands, steady eye contact, small measured movements.
- Screen presence: academic authority with a faintly clinical warmth.

## Nicole

### Character Function

Nicole represents the control layer's confidence. She starts as the person who believes the system is working because the logs say it is working. Her fear is powerful because she resists it at first. She is not cruel; she is professional, tired, and trained to treat Oscar as a subject until the same unreality reaches her.

### Personality

- Procedural, responsible, and controlled.
- Values logs, protocols, and clean explanations.
- Protective of the system because the system gives her identity and order.
- Skeptical of Cindy's panic until the evidence becomes undeniable.
- Fear shows through small breaks: longer pauses, less precise speech, tightened jaw.
- Her arc moves from command to uncertainty.

### Physical Outline

- Age: early 30s.
- Build: average height, composed posture, practical rather than glamorous.
- Face: oval or heart-shaped face, focused eyes, tired but disciplined expression.
- Hair: dark brown hair in a neat ponytail or low bun.
- Skin: medium or light-medium natural skin tone, realistic texture.
- Wardrobe: navy sweater or dark simple top, headset around neck, ID badge optional.
- Body language: hands near keyboard, shoulders squared, controlled movements.
- Screen presence: calm shift supervisor in a cold technical environment.

## Cindy

### Character Function

Cindy is the first person in the control room to feel the loop directly. She is exhausted, more intuitive than Nicole, and more willing to trust her body when something feels wrong. Her fear pulls the control room out of procedure and into the same human panic Oscar felt.

### Personality

- Sharp, tired, observant, and emotionally honest.
- More intuitive than Nicole; notices small sensory details first.
- Uses dry humor when exhausted.
- Less invested in the system's authority, so she questions it faster.
- Her fear is immediate but not weak; she pushes for proof.
- Her arc moves from fatigue to alarm to existential clarity.

### Physical Outline

- Age: late 20s.
- Build: average or slight build, slouched from long hours at a console.
- Face: rounder face or soft square jaw, expressive eyes, visible tiredness.
- Hair: short black hair, slightly messy, practical cut.
- Skin: light-medium to medium natural skin tone, realistic texture.
- Wardrobe: gray hoodie under a dark utility vest or black overshirt, practical work clothes.
- Props: energy drink can, keyboard, log screen.
- Body language: leans close to monitors, rubs eyes, fidgets with can, reacts faster than Nicole.

## Character Consistency Checklist

- Generate each character reference before generating scene images.
- Pick one best reference per character and reuse it through Artlist Studio tags/capture.
- Do not change Oscar's hair length, Serena's bob, Nicole's ponytail, or Cindy's short hair.
- Keep Oscar's home outfit and university outfit distinct.
- Keep Serena's clothing elegant and dark in all layers.
- Keep Nicole cleaner and more composed than Cindy.
- Keep Cindy's energy drink can as her repeated-loop prop.
- Use expression variants only after the base character is locked.
