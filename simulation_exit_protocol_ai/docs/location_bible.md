# Simulation Exit Protocol Location Bible

Use these prompts to create and lock the major environments in Artlist Studio before generating character shots. Start with wide establishing images, save the strongest version of each location, then reuse that location reference for closer scene frames.

## Global Location Style

Realistic modern psychological thriller, cinematic horizontal 16:9, natural architectural detail, believable lived-in spaces, subtle unease, no futuristic sci-fi design, no fantasy elements, no neon cyberpunk, no exaggerated horror set dressing, no visible crew or cameras.

## Location 01: Oscar's Apartment Interior

### Purpose

Oscar's apartment should feel ordinary, young, neat, and slightly lonely. It is not luxury or designer-perfect, but it should look organized: a university-age young man living alone, keeping his space controlled, quiet, and minimal. The room should feel calm enough that small glitches become obvious.

### Opening Establishing Shot - Large Slow Pan

Use this as the first locked location image for Oscar's apartment. This should be location-only, with no Oscar visible, so it can become the master reference for later bedroom, phone, kitchen, coffee, mirror, and reset shots.

Prompt:

> Wide cinematic establishing shot of a small modern student apartment belonging to a young university-age man, realistic neat studio layout, camera positioned near the entrance as if beginning a slow panning shot across the whole room, neatly made or mostly straightened bed on one side, compact kitchenette on the other, small tidy desk with closed laptop, organized charger cable, stacked university books and one notebook, clean chair, white coffee mug near the kitchen counter, narrow hallway mirror visible in the background, simple floor lamp casting a long shadow, half-open blinds over a window with faint city light outside, muted gray and off-white walls, dark wood or laminate floor, black electronics, minimal organized belongings, lonely late-night atmosphere, dim phone-like blue light mixed with weak streetlight, grounded modern psychological thriller, subtle unease, cinematic horizontal 16:9, realistic architecture, no people, no futuristic design, no horror decorations, no fantasy elements, not messy.

Animation direction for image-to-video:

> Slow left-to-right pan across the apartment, beginning near the doorway, passing the neat bed and tidy desk, ending on the kitchenette and coffee mug. Keep motion smooth and restrained, like an ordinary room being quietly observed.

### Visual Identity

- Small modern apartment or studio unit.
- Neatly made or mostly straightened bed visible from the living/kitchen area.
- Compact kitchen with a coffee mug, kettle or coffee maker, sink, and small counter.
- Tidy desk or side table with laptop, organized cable, stacked books, notebook, and phone charger.
- Half-open blinds that create strong morning light lines.
- Neutral colors: gray, off-white, muted blue, dark wood, black electronics.
- Atmosphere: ordinary, private, controlled, emotionally quiet.

### Wide Establishing Prompt - Night Interior

Oscar's small modern student apartment at night, wide establishing shot from the corner of the room, neat bed, small clean kitchen counter, tidy desk with closed laptop and organized charger cable, stacked university books and one coffee mug, half-open blinds, dim city light outside, phone glow as the only bright source, realistic organized young man's space, quiet loneliness, grounded psychological thriller, cinematic horizontal 16:9, minimal belongings, subtle unease, not messy.

### Wide Establishing Prompt - Morning Interior

Oscar's small modern student apartment in morning sunlight, wide establishing shot showing neat bed, compact kitchen, white coffee mug on counter, tidy desk with laptop and stacked university books, half-open blinds casting striped light across the floor, ordinary organized young man's space, warm but slightly unsettling calm, realistic modern psychological thriller, cinematic horizontal 16:9, minimal belongings, no futuristic elements, not messy.

### Continuity Notes

- Keep the apartment layout consistent: bed, kitchen, desk, hallway mirror, lamp, and blinds should feel like parts of one compact space.
- The night version should feel cold and screen-lit.
- The morning version should feel warmer, which makes the glitches more disturbing.
- After the reset, use the same apartment but make the light softer and more reassuring.

## Location 02: Oscar's Apartment Exterior

### Purpose

The exterior should establish Oscar as a university-age young man living near campus or in modest student housing. It should feel realistic and slightly anonymous, not cinematic in an obvious way.

### First Scene Establishing Shot - Exterior Drone Approach

Use this as the opening shot if you want to begin outside before entering Oscar's private world. This is a scene-starting frame, not just a clean empty location plate, because Oscar appears opening the door.

Prompt:

> Wide aerial drone-style establishing shot outside a modest student apartment building in a realistic university neighborhood at night, camera high and slowly descending toward the building entrance and second-floor windows, quiet sidewalk, bike racks, parked compact cars, cool bluish-white moonlight and pale neutral streetlight, most apartment windows dark or only faintly lit with dim cool white interior light, no warm golden window glow, no bright yellow lights, no cozy amber lighting, Oscar visible as a small figure opening the building door or apartment entrance, early-20s slim young man in casual home clothes or dark jacket, the world feels ordinary and safe but slightly isolated, grounded modern psychological thriller, cinematic horizontal 16:9, desaturated natural colors, realistic architecture, no futuristic design, no horror decorations, no rain-soaked noir style.

Animation direction for image-to-video:

> Slow descending drone-like push toward the apartment building, drifting from a wide neighborhood view toward Oscar opening the door. Keep the motion smooth, quiet, and observational, as if the camera is watching him from outside before the story moves into his room.

Lighting rule:

> Match the interior's bluish-white night feeling. The exterior should be cool, dim, and desaturated. Avoid golden apartment windows, orange streetlights, cozy warmth, or high-contrast glowing windows.

### Visual Identity

- Modest mid-rise apartment building or student housing block.
- Urban university neighborhood.
- Small balconies, mailboxes, bike racks, narrow sidewalk, parked cars or scooters.
- Night for the opening drone shot; early morning or late afternoon for later transition shots.
- Slightly worn but safe and ordinary.
- Not luxury, not dystopian, not abandoned.

### Wide Establishing Prompt - Exterior Morning

Modest student apartment building in a realistic university neighborhood, wide exterior establishing shot, early morning light, small balconies, bike racks, narrow sidewalk, parked compact cars, a few students walking in the distance, ordinary young adult housing, slightly worn but safe, grounded psychological thriller tone, cinematic horizontal 16:9, natural colors, subtle unease, no futuristic design.

### Wide Establishing Prompt - Oscar Leaving

Wide exterior shot of a modest student apartment building entrance in morning light, Oscar's figure small in frame leaving quickly with a dark jacket and black backpack, bike racks and parked cars nearby, quiet university neighborhood, realistic young man's life, understated psychological thriller atmosphere, cinematic horizontal 16:9.

### Continuity Notes

- Use the same exterior if you need a transition between apartment and university.
- Oscar should look small against ordinary architecture, emphasizing how normal the world still appears.

## Location 03: University Exterior

### Purpose

The university should feel credible and modern, not gothic or fantasy. Serena's lecture and Room 214 should belong to an institution that could plausibly host a controversial livestream talk while still looking ordinary in daylight.

### Visual Identity

- Modern university campus building, glass and concrete or brick and glass.
- Students moving between classes.
- Clean but impersonal academic atmosphere.
- Daylight, neutral weather, restrained color palette.
- Public reality should feel normal before Oscar enters Room 214.

### Wide Establishing Prompt - Campus Arrival

Realistic modern university campus exterior, wide establishing shot of an academic building with glass and concrete facade, students walking between classes, trees and benches, daylight, ordinary campus life, Oscar as a small anxious figure approaching in dark jacket with black backpack, grounded psychological thriller, cinematic horizontal 16:9, natural colors, no futuristic sci-fi architecture.

### Wide Establishing Prompt - Building Entrance

Wide cinematic shot of a modern university building entrance, glass doors, concrete columns, notice boards, students passing casually, Oscar entering quickly with dark jacket and black backpack, daylight realism, quiet tension beneath normal campus activity, psychological thriller tone, horizontal 16:9.

### Continuity Notes

- Keep the university bright and public, contrasting with Oscar's private panic.
- Background students should look normal and unaware.
- Avoid making the campus look too elite, too old, or too stylized.

## Location 04: University Hallway

### Purpose

The hallway is the bridge between normal public life and Serena's private room. It should feel institutional, fluorescent, and slightly too long.

### Visual Identity

- Long academic corridor.
- Fluorescent ceiling lights.
- Room numbers on doors.
- Bulletin boards, faded posters, lockers or office doors.
- Students passing in background.
- Room 214 should be clearly visible in close or medium-wide shots.

### Wide Establishing Prompt - Hallway

Long realistic university hallway, wide establishing shot, fluorescent ceiling lights, office doors with room numbers, bulletin boards with academic posters, muted institutional colors, students blurred in the distance, Oscar walking quickly in dark jacket with black backpack, anxious body language, grounded psychological thriller, cinematic horizontal 16:9, subtle unease.

### Room 214 Approach Prompt

Medium-wide realistic university hallway shot focused on a door marked Room 214, door slightly ajar, fluorescent light, muted walls, academic posters nearby, Oscar approaching cautiously from the side of frame, dark jacket and black backpack, quiet institutional tension, cinematic horizontal 16:9.

### Continuity Notes

- Room 214 should be on the same hallway style as the campus building.
- The hallway should not be empty at first; normal people nearby make Oscar's fear feel isolated.
- When Oscar reaches Room 214, reduce background activity and make the sound/space feel quieter.

## Location 05: Room 214

### Purpose

Room 214 should feel like a real academic office/seminar room that has been quietly repurposed by Serena. It should not look like a secret lab. The tension comes from how ordinary it is.

### Visual Identity

- Small university seminar room or professor's office.
- Desk with folders, papers, and a leather notebook.
- Bookshelves, old safe on shelf, two chairs, worn tabletop.
- Fluorescent light mixed with muted daylight.
- No advanced technology except maybe a laptop or projector remote.
- The red and blue cases appear symbolic because the room is so plain.

### Wide Establishing Prompt - Room 214 Interior

Small realistic university office-seminar room, wide establishing shot, worn desk, two chairs, bookshelves, papers, leather folder, small metal safe on a shelf, muted daylight mixed with fluorescent ceiling light, institutional walls, ordinary academic clutter, no secret laboratory equipment, calm unsettling atmosphere, grounded psychological thriller, cinematic horizontal 16:9.

### Serena At Desk Establishing Prompt

Wide realistic shot inside Room 214, Professor Serena Voss seated calmly at a worn desk with notes and a leather folder, two chairs facing her, bookshelves and a small safe behind her, muted university office light, Oscar entering cautiously from the doorway, psychological thriller tension, cinematic horizontal 16:9.

### Continuity Notes

- Keep the room plain and credible.
- The small safe should be visible in wide shots so its later use feels planted.
- Serena's calm presence should contrast with Oscar's anxious movement.
